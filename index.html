Oopbuy spreadsheet guide

Turn a long list into a useful shortlist.

Use the same research method for a pair of shoes, a hoodie, or a small accessory.

01

Choose one category

Begin with an item type and a clear purpose. Separate everyday sneakers, seasonal layers, sportswear, and accessories so unrelated products do not compete in the same comparison.

02

Capture comparable details

Save the listing address, selected variant, measurements, stated materials, seller name, and date checked. Mark missing information rather than filling the gap with an assumption.

03

Review before paying

Reopen the listing to confirm price and availability. Review delivery charges, return conditions, destination restrictions, and any purchase protections that apply to your order.

Oopbuy shopping checklist

Read beyond the product title.

Fit is a measurement question.

For T-shirts, hoodies, jackets, and sets, compare the seller's measurements with a similar garment you own. Check whether the measurements describe the garment or the wearer's body.

  • Clothing: chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length, and total length.
  • Pants and shorts: waist, rise, thigh width, and inseam.
  • Headwear: internal circumference and adjustment range.
  • Footwear: the seller's size chart and any model-specific fit notes.

A photograph answers only some questions.

An Oopbuy QC checklist can help you review visible color, stitching, shape, and the selected variant. Photos cannot establish every material property, long-term durability, or authenticity.

  • Match the photographed variant to the one you intend to order.
  • Look for clear views of seams, closures, labels, and relevant dimensions.
  • Request missing information before treating a detail as confirmed.
  • Keep seller claims separate from independently supported evidence.

Clear answers

Before you follow a find.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet research and product comparisons.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product research sheet that groups shopping finds and listing links, often alongside prices, size notes, seller information, and quality-check references. The usefulness of a sheet depends on the accuracy and freshness of its entries.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and an educational comparison guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS.

Does a spreadsheet entry prove product authenticity?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name, product image, or quality-check photograph does not independently verify authenticity. Check seller evidence, provenance, and relevant purchase protections.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check the measurement units and allow for the stated measurement tolerance rather than relying only on letter sizes.

What belongs in a shopping cost estimate?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service charges where applicable, international shipping, and potential taxes or duties. Confirm current amounts and destination requirements before paying.

Is this an official Oopbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial directory. It does not represent Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès.
